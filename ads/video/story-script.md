Status: **proposed by Claude on 2026-11-17, not yet approved by the founder, nothing produced.** The second video to test, after the quiz video in this folder.

About 30 seconds, 9:16 (1080 x 1920), first-person voiceover in Hinglish with burnt-in captions. Style: 3D cartoon, like the competitor ads the founder shared, made in an AI video tool or by a designer. Claude cannot generate the footage or the voice.

## Why this story

The competitor stories are "she tried everything, then found us". Ours has one thing they do not: **types**. Two friends follow the same diet; it works for one and not the other, because their symptoms match different types. That is the whole pitch in one picture, and it is not a copy of anyone's ad.

## What is deliberately left out

- **No thinner Neha at the end.** Shot 7 shows her at ease, not a smaller body. The site makes no weight promise on its timeline ([D-022](../../docs/DECISIONS.md)), and a before-and-after body is the kind of picture Meta restricts.
- **No "PCOS reversed" or "cured".** She says her symptoms *matched* a type, the wording the doctor chose.
- **No doctor or dietitian character.** The product does not name one.
